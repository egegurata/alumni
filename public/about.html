About the Alumni Tracking System

This system was architected and developed by Ege Gürkan Atalay (egegurkanatalay@ogr.iu.edu.tr) for the Web Programming (YBSB3001) course taught by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Emre Akadal at Istanbul University, Department of Management Information Systems (Fall 2026).

Architectural Highlights (Weeks 01–06)

  • Week 01 (Inception & Stack): Node.js (Express.js) back-end paired with PostgreSQL 16 in Docker Compose.
  • Week 02 (Routing): Parameterized routes (/hello, /hello/:name, /sum/:a/:b with strict numeric validation returning 400 Bad Request on non-numeric segments) and static pages.
  • Week 03 (HTTP Verbs & CRUD): Full RESTful CRUD on /api/users with isAlumni and graduationYear fields, /api/health with live uptime, 204 No Content on delete, and interactive Swagger UI at /api/swagger.
  • Week 04 (MVC Architecture): Strict separation between Models (data & validation rules), Controllers (API & Web), Views (HTML templates), and Routes, including the Announcement module.
  • Week 05 (Database & ORM / Persistence): PostgreSQL 16 service with a named Docker volume (pgdata), Database Design Document (ddd.md), SQL migrations, factories, idempotent seeder, and 409 Conflict enforcement on duplicate emails.
  • Week 06 (Relational Data & Advanced Routing): One-to-Many job_postings and Many-to-Many applications join table, nested routes (/api/users/:id/jobs, /api/jobs/:id/applications), LEFT JOIN eager loading on GET /api/users/:id, and ON DELETE CASCADE integrity.
